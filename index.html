How to Register

STEP-BY-STEP
INSTRUCTIONS

  1. 1 Visit sih.gov.in and shortlist a Problem Statement your team can solve before registration opens.
  2. 2 Form a team of exactly 6 members — 1 Team Leader + 5 Members. All from BCA III or V Sem.
  3. 3 Ensure your team has at least 1 female member. This is mandatory — no exceptions.
  4. 4 The Team Leader submits the Google Form for the full team. Link will be shared by college soon.
  5. 5 Keep all member details and your Problem Statement ID (e.g., SIH26001) ready before filling the form.
